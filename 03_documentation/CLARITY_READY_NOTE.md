# FreeZone — CLARITY-ready Design Note

FreeZone is designed to be compatible with the principles of digital asset market-structure regulation by separating operational evidence from digital asset activity.

## What FreeZone does not do

FreeZone does not:

- Issue a new token.
- Custody user funds.
- Operate as an exchange.
- Operate as a broker/dealer.
- Store private business documents on-chain.

## What FreeZone does

FreeZone uses Avalanche as a verifiable recordkeeping layer.

It records:

- Document hashes.
- Timestamps.
- Reputation attestations.
- Payment intent status.

Documents remain off-chain.

Payments can be routed through external regulated providers or sandbox payment APIs.

## Why this matters

The product keeps a clean separation between:

```txt
Operational evidence
Digital asset infrastructure
Payment execution
```

This allows FreeZone to demonstrate blockchain utility without turning the product into a speculative asset or custody platform.

## Suggested phrasing

Use:

```txt
CLARITY-ready by design
Aligned with CLARITY Act principles
Designed for regulated settlement integrations
```

Avoid:

```txt
Fully compliant with the CLARITY Act
Legally certified
Regulatory approved
```

Those require formal legal review.
